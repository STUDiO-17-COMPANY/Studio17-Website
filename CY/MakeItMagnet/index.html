Common Questions

How long does delivery take?

Production typically takes 2-3 business days. Shipping via Akis Express or Travel Express usually takes another 24 hours to reach your nearest pick-up point anywhere in Cyprus.

Is there a minimum order?

We recommend a minimum set of 4 magnets to make shipping worthwhile, but there is no strict minimum!

How do I send my photos?

Once you confirm your order via DM, you can send photos via WhatsApp (Document mode) or email to ensure the highest quality print.
