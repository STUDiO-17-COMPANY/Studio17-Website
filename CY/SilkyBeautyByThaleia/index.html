Meet Thaleia

With over a decade of experience in the high-end aesthetics industry, Thaleia established her Limassol studio with a singular vision: to create a space where clinical results meet spa-like tranquility.

Unlike standardized clinics, Thaleia adopts a curator’s approach. Every facial, every brow shape, and every treatment plan is sketched out specifically for the individual client. Her expertise lies in understanding facial symmetry and skin biology, ensuring results that look effortlessly natural.
