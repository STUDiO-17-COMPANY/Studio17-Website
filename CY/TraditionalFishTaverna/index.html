Our Philosophy

Simply Fresh,
Simply Grilled.

In Latsia, "Η πιατέλα του Ψαρά" has become synonymous with authentic Cypriot hospitality. Our decor is inspired by the whitewashed Cycladic style, but our soul is purely Cypriot.

We believe the best meze starts with the freshest catch. No heavy sauces, no complications—just olive oil, lemon, charcoal, and the sea.
